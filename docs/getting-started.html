Getting Started with Hearth

Welcome Home

Hearth is a space designed for genuine connection with your closest people. Unlike traditional social media, there are no algorithms, no infinite scrolls, and no pressure to perform. Just you and your Kin.

1. Set Up Your Profile

Your profile is your home base. Here you can:

  • Set a display name that your friends know you by.
  • Share life updates like your job, location, or relationship status.
  • Control exactly who sees what information.

2. Find Your Key

Every Hearth user has a unique Hearth Key. This is how you connect with others. You can find yours at the top of your Profile page.

HEARTH-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX

3. Add Kin

To connect with someone:

  1. Go to your Profile.
  2. Click + Add Kin.
  3. Enter their Hearth Key.
  4. Choose a tier: Kin or Inner Circle.

4. Share a Pulse

Head to the Pulse tab to share what's on your mind. It's a simple text update to let your people know how you're doing.
