The Hearth Key System

What is a Hearth Key?

A Hearth Key is a unique, 16-character identifier assigned to you when you join. It looks like this:

HEARTH-AB12-CD34-EF56

Unlike usernames, Hearth Keys are not easily guessed, adding a layer of intentionality to who you connect with.

Sharing Your Key

You can share your key in two ways from your Profile:

  1. Copy Key: Copies the text to your clipboard to paste in messages.
  2. Share via QR: Generates a QR code that friends can scan directly from their device.

Connecting with Keys

To add someone, you must enter their exact Hearth Key. This ensures you are connecting with the right person and prevents spam or unwanted requests from strangers.
