Privacy Controls

Granular Privacy

Hearth is built on the principle that you should have complete control over your data. We offer granular privacy settings for your profile and updates.

Global Settings

In your Profile under "Privacy & Visibility", you can toggle:

  • Allow Contacts to Find Me: If disabled, people cannot find you via phone contacts (if implemented in future updates).
  • Show Read Receipts: Decide if Kin can see when you've read their Parlor messages.
  • Cloud Backup: Manage if your data is synced to the cloud.

Life Updates Visibility

For each piece of life information (Job, Location, Relationship), you can set specific visibility:

  • Private: Only you can see this.
  • Inner Circle: Visible only to your closest connections.
  • All Kin: Visible to everyone you've connected with.
