Pulse Visibility

The Pulse Feed

Pulse is the heartbeat of your network. It's a place for transient, authentic updates about your day, mood, or thoughts.

Who Sees My Pulse?

When you post a Pulse, it is visible to All Kin by default. Currently, Pulse updates are broadcast to everyone you have accepted as a connection.

Relationship Tiers

While Pulse is generally visible to your network, classifying your connections helps you manage your relationships mentally.

  • Inner Circle: Your closest friends and family. They might see more detailed profile info.
  • Kin: Friends and acquaintances you want to stay in touch with.

Note: Future updates may allow restricting individual Pulse posts to Inner Circle only.
