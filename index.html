Respect & Privacy

A Different Kind of Social Space

Hearth is not a platform for "content creators." It is a space for connection. There are no "Followers," only "Kin." There are no "Likes," only "Acknowledged" or "Heartfelt" responses.

The Three Golden Rules

1

Consent by Default

Every data point is private until you explicitly toggle it to "Visible to Kin." Your information belongs to you.

🔒
2

Linearity over Algorithms

Content appears chronologically. When you reach the end, the app tells you: "You're all caught up. Go enjoy your day."

📅
3

Zero External Interop

No "Share to X" buttons. No "Login with Google." What happens in the Hearth stays in the Hearth.
