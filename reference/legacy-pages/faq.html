Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my theme?
Go to your Profile and scroll down to "Additional Settings". Use the dropdown menu under "Theme" to select from options like Oatmeal, Clay, Ocean, Forest, or Charcoal.
Can I delete a Pulse?
Currently, Pulses are designed to be transient. They may expire or be pushed down by new updates. Manual deletion is a feature planned for the future.
What is the "Sunset Timer"?
The Sunset Timer allows you to set specific hours during which the app interface will dim, helping you wind down in the evening and reducing blue light exposure.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We prioritize your privacy. Your data is stored securely, and we do not sell your information to advertisers. You have full control over who sees your profile updates.
How do I remove a Kin connection?
Go to your Profile, find the person in your "My Kin" list, and click the "Remove" button on their card.
